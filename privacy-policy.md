# eFleckt Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: July 14, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

At Naylormade, LLC ("we," "us," "our," or "eFleckt"), we are deeply committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal information when you use the eFleckt mobile application, our website, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

eFleckt is a personal memory and meaning-making app built around life chapters, cast members (the important people in your life), and voice and text journal entries. We understand that the content you create is deeply personal, and we treat your data with the utmost care and respect.

By using eFleckt, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use our Service.

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our Service:

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

**Account Information:**

- Name
- Email address
- Date of birth

You sign in through Google or Apple authentication. We do not collect or store passwords — authentication is handled entirely by your chosen provider using industry-standard OAuth protocols.

**Voice Recordings and Text Entries:**

- Audio recordings you create within the app
- Text entries you write within the app
- Metadata associated with entries (date, time, duration, entry type)
- Chapter assignments and temporal context you provide or confirm

**Life Organization Data:**

- Chapter names, date ranges, and descriptions
- Cast members (names, relationships, notes, and character studies)
- Locations (names, descriptions, and associations with entries)
- Emotions and tags associated with entries

### 2.2 Information Generated by AI Processing

When you create an entry, our AI pipeline generates additional data derived from your content:

- Transcriptions of voice recordings
- Emotional analysis of vocal tone and content
- Structured summaries and insights from your entries
- Temporal classification (when an entry's events occurred)
- Entity summaries synthesized across multiple entries about a person or place
- **Whole-life narratives** — a 5-paragraph chronological life story and six per-emotion-family reflections, synthesized by reading all of your entries together. These power the "Your Story" surface and the Emotions tab on your About Me page. They regenerate on your explicit request, and automatically become free to refresh after 90 days or 15 new entries since the last generation
- Semantic embeddings (numerical representations of your entry content used for search and connections — not human-readable)

### 2.3 Information Collected Automatically

**Device Information:** Device type, operating system version, screen dimensions, app version

**Location (optional):** When you record a present-moment entry, your device's GPS coordinates may be captured once to help identify the location. This requires your explicit permission and is never tracked in the background.

**Usage Analytics:** App features used, navigation patterns, crash reports, performance data (collected via PostHog)

**Website Data:** When you visit our website, standard web server logs and analytics data may be collected by our hosting provider (Vercel)

### 2.4 Information We Do NOT Collect

- **Passwords** — we use OAuth sign-in only
- **Background location tracking** — we never track your location in the background. When you record a present-moment entry, we may capture a one-time GPS coordinate to help identify where you are. You control this through your device's location permissions, and you can edit or remove any location from an entry at any time.
- **Contacts or phone data** — we never access your device contacts, call logs, or messages

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide, maintain, improve, and protect our Service:

### 3.1 Core Service Functions

- **Account Management:** Creating and managing your account, authentication, and customer support
- **Transcription:** Converting voice recordings to text using AssemblyAI's Universal-3 Pro speech-to-text model
- **Emotional Analysis:** Analyzing vocal tone using Google Gemini's multimodal audio analysis technology
- **AI Synthesis:** Generating structured summaries, insights, temporal classification, and entity summaries using Anthropic's Claude language models
- **Semantic Search:** Creating numerical embeddings of your content using Google Gemini's embedding model to enable connections and search across your entries
- **Memory Organization:** Organizing entries into chapters, associating cast members and locations, and presenting your personal timeline
- **Subscription Management:** Processing subscriptions and credit pack purchases through RevenueCat (iOS and Android) and Stripe (web)

### 3.2 What We Will NEVER Do

We will never:

- Sell your personal data or recordings to third parties
- Use your voice recordings or entry content to train AI models
- Share your content publicly or use it for marketing without consent
- Share your data with advertisers or data brokers
- Use your data for purposes unrelated to providing eFleckt

### 3.3 Your Consent Controls

Before any AI processing of your content, eFleckt asks for your consent on a dedicated consent screen — separate from your acceptance of these policies — with two independent choices:

- **AI synthesis** (transcription, summaries, organization): required to use eFleckt's core features
- **Emotion analysis** (vocal tone and content-based emotional insight): optional, off by default, and entirely your choice

Each consent decision is recorded with a timestamp and a cryptographic fingerprint of the exact text you agreed to, so there is never ambiguity about what you consented to. You can withdraw either consent at any time in Settings; withdrawal takes effect immediately for all future processing.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

### 5.1 Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored on Supabase's infrastructure, which runs on Amazon Web Services (AWS) in the US West (Oregon, us-west-2) region. Our website is hosted on Vercel. Audio files are stored in Supabase Storage. All database records, including entries, chapters, cast members, and AI-generated content, are stored in Supabase's managed PostgreSQL database.

### 5.2 Offline Storage

eFleckt caches data locally on your device using an encrypted SQLite database to enable limited offline functionality. This local cache is stored only on your device and is cleared when you delete the app.

### 5.3 Security Measures

**Technical Safeguards:**

- **Encryption at rest:** All data is encrypted using AES-256, with per-project encryption keys protected by FIPS 140-2 compliant hardware security modules (HSMs)
- **Encryption in transit:** All data transmission uses TLS 1.2 or higher with modern cipher suites
- **SOC 2 Type 2:** Our infrastructure provider (Supabase) maintains SOC 2 Type 2 certification with annual independent audits
- **Payment security:** All payment processing is handled by Stripe (PCI Level 1 certified) and RevenueCat — we never see, store, or process your credit card information
- OAuth-based authentication (no passwords stored)
- Row-level security policies on all database tables (each user can only access their own data)
- API keys and secrets stored securely in server-side environment variables
- Audio files stored with per-user access controls

While we implement robust security measures, no system is completely secure. We describe our limits honestly in Section 5.4 and in "Our Privacy Commitments to You" below.

### 5.4 Security Incidents

If we become aware of a security breach affecting your personal data, we will notify affected users without undue delay where the breach is likely to result in a risk to you, and we will notify regulators where and when applicable law requires (including within 72 hours where GDPR Article 33 applies, and as required by California Civil Code §1798.82). Notification will describe what happened, what data was affected, what we have done about it, and what you should do.

## 6. Data Retention

- **Active accounts:** Data is retained for the life of your account
- **Deleted entries:** Removed from active systems promptly; removed from backups within 90 days
- **Deleted accounts:** Deleting your account removes your content from active systems immediately; residual copies in encrypted backups are purged within 90 days
- **Payment records:** Retained for 7 years as required for tax and financial compliance
- **AI processing logs:** Transient — not retained after processing is complete

## 11. AI and Automated Processing

eFleckt uses artificial intelligence to enhance your experience:

- **Transcription:** AssemblyAI Universal-3 Pro converts voice recordings to text
- **Emotion Analysis:** Google Gemini analyzes vocal prosody to detect emotional tone from the audio signal
- **Synthesis:** Anthropic Claude generates structured summaries, insights, and classifications from your entries
- **Temporal Classification:** AI determines when the events in your entry occurred and suggests chapter assignments
- **Entity Summaries:** AI synthesizes information across entries to create summaries about people and places in your life
- **About Me Whole-Life Synthesis:** Anthropic Claude reads your entries together to compose a 5-paragraph chronological life story and six per-emotion-family reflections for your About Me page. This synthesis is distinct from the per-entry and per-entity processing above — your full entry timeline is sent together as a single batch so the narrative reads in life-time order. The synthesis runs when you tap "Refresh" and is free to regenerate after 90 days OR 15 new entries since the last generation. Every past version is preserved so you can view the history
- **Embeddings:** Google Gemini generates semantic embeddings to enable search and connections across your entries

**Important limitations:**

- AI-generated content is not always accurate and may misinterpret your meaning
- eFleckt is not a substitute for professional therapy, medical advice, or counseling
- You can review and edit all AI-generated content (summaries, classifications, chapter assignments)
- We do not use your recordings or entries to train any AI models — your content is processed only to generate results for you

## 12. Cookies and Tracking

**What we track:** Product analytics include event data such as screens viewed, features used, and aggregate usage patterns. We use this data solely to improve the product. Analytics events never contain the content of your entries, the names of your cast members, the names of your locations, or any other personal narrative content. Your story stays yours — analytics only tells us which buttons you tap, not what you say.

**Website:** Our website (hosted on Vercel) may use:

- Essential cookies required for website functionality
- Analytics (PostHog) to understand website usage — you can opt out

We do NOT use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking, or retargeting on either the app or website.
